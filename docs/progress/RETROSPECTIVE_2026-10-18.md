## Method and limits

- A green PR or successful one-off experiment does not automatically satisfy another task. We compare the task's actual acceptance text and dependencies, not similarity of implementation names.
- 'Criterion met' here means a task was already recorded as `done` in the frozen ledger; prior linked evidence is not independently re-audited in depth. 'Partially met' means new code/evidence is relevant but insufficient to meet the entire original criterion. 'Not applicable' means the reviewed 16–18 October work does not justify that task's completion.
- Private local evidence is supported by the dated public index and operator reports, **not by a GitHub-runner rerun of model inference**. Model licences, signed gold masks, full throughput and paid billing remain unproven.
- No task state, frozen denominator, release unit or milestone gate has been modified by this retrospective. The nearest GPU-free task to finish is **S03-A03**, with its exact criterion: **'Pixel-level tests prove removal without deleting background-colored product regions'**.
